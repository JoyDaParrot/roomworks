# RoomWorks — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 September 2026**

RoomWorks is a multiplayer virtual reality game. This policy explains what information the app processes, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. It covers the RoomWorks application itself, not just this website.

> **What changed in this version:** RoomWorks now has a drawing pen, so players can draw shapes in the air that everybody in the room can see. Anything you draw is something you have created and other people will see, so it is covered by the same rules as anything else you say or show. See **Drawings and other things you make**.
>
> **What changed in the version before this one:** RoomWorks began letting players report each other, and a report includes a short voice clip. An earlier version of this policy said voice was never recorded or stored, and that there was no way to report something said over voice. Both of those changed, and the sections below describe what actually happens now.

| **Text chat messages** | Text chat with other players. | Sent to other players in your room. Not stored by us after the session ends. |

## Microphone access

RoomWorks requests microphone permission so you can talk to other players. Voice is transmitted live to others in your room while you are connected.

**Your voice is moderated, to keep RoomWorks safe.** Here is exactly what that means.

We do not record your conversations. Nothing is transcribed, nothing is archived, and nobody listens in on voice chat.

To make reporting possible, the app keeps the **most recent ten seconds** of voice while you are in a room — yours, and each person you can hear. It stays in memory on your own device, is continuously overwritten, and is never saved to storage.

That ten seconds has one use. If somebody submits a report, it is attached to that report so a moderator can hear what actually happened. **If nobody reports anything, it is never uploaded, and it is gone as soon as it is overwritten or you leave the room.** Silence is not captured, so there is often nothing there at all.

The settings menu shows a live meter of your own microphone level, so you can see whether the app is hearing you. That is drawn on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Voice transmission can be turned off from the in-game settings menu, and you can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in your headset's system settings — the rest of the game will continue to work. Muted means nothing is transmitted and nothing is buffered.

## Reporting a player

You can report another player from the social menu by choosing them, picking a reason, and optionally describing what happened.

For most reasons, submitting a report captures the following **at the moment you press submit**:

- **Ten seconds of your voice**, and **ten seconds of theirs**
- **A screenshot from your viewpoint**, and **a reconstructed view from theirs**, both of the virtual world
- **Both usernames**, the reason you picked, and anything you typed
- The time, and whether other players have recently reported the same person

**Both screenshots are of the game world only.** RoomWorks does not request camera permission and never takes a photograph of you or your surroundings. The view from the reported player's position is generated inside the game and is labelled as reconstructed.

**Some reasons collect nothing extra.** Reports about an inappropriate bio, username, or profile picture attach no audio and no screenshots, because the thing being complained about is the profile itself.

Reports go to RoomWorks moderators. They are not shown to other players, and **the person you report is not told who reported them**.

You can submit one report every 30 seconds, and one report about the same player per 24 hours.

## Accounts

RoomWorks now has accounts. You can still play as a guest without one.

If you choose to create an account, you verify yourself in one of three ways:

- **Discord** — we receive your Discord ID, your Discord username, and the email address on that account. We ask Discord only for basic profile information and your email address. We cannot see your servers, your friends, or your messages.
- **Meta** — we receive your Meta account ID and confirmation from Meta that the account is allowed to use the app.
- **Email and password** — we receive the email address you enter. Passwords are handled by Unity Authentication, and we never see or store them in readable form.

With an account we store your username, your email address, and your profile picture, so your profile follows you between sessions and devices.

If you play as a guest, the server creates a temporary identity with a randomly generated name. Guest records are deleted 24 hours after you leave, unless that guest has been banned.

RoomWorks does not collect a real name, a postal address, or any payment details.

## Drawings and other things you make

RoomWorks has a drawing pen. Lines you draw with it appear in the air and everybody else in the room sees them, in the same way they see your avatar, hear your voice, and read your text chat.

**What happens to a drawing.** The lines are sent straight to the other players in the room. We do not store them, they are not saved between sessions, and they are not uploaded anywhere. When you leave the room, or the room ends, the drawing is gone. A pen only keeps a limited number of recent lines, so older ones disappear as you keep drawing.

**A drawing is not checked automatically.** Text chat is passed through a word filter before it is sent. **A drawing is not** — there is no automatic check on what a line looks like or what it spells out. What appears is what the person drew.

**Drawings are still covered by moderation.** A drawing is content you have published to everybody in the room, and the rules that apply to what you say apply to what you draw. If somebody reports a player, the report can include an in-game screenshot, and a drawing visible at that moment will be in it. A moderator can then act on it in the same way as any other report, up to and including a ban. Do not draw anything you would not be allowed to say.

**If somebody draws something you do not want to see**, you can report them, and you can block them. Blocking is described under **Reporting a player**.

## Moderation

We keep a record of accounts and guests that have been banned, along with the reason and when the ban ends, so that a ban can be enforced if that person returns. Moderators can add notes about players, and moderator actions are logged. Notes and logs are visible to moderators, not to other players.

We also keep reports submitted by players, and the evidence attached to them, so they can be reviewed. If a moderator sends you a message about a report, the app collects it the next time you play.

If you register an account while banned as a guest, the ban carries over to the new account.

## Third-party services

**Epic Online Services (Epic Games)** provides the multiplayer networking and the voice chat. Data described above passes through Epic's service to reach other players, including your IP address and connection details. Epic's privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy

**Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies)** provides sign-in, account storage, profile pictures, saved settings, ban records, and player reports including their evidence. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

**Discord** is involved only if you choose to verify your account with Discord. Discord's privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy

RoomWorks is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta's handling of your account and purchase information is governed by Meta's own privacy policy and is outside our control.

## How long information is kept

Voice, chat, drawings, position, and movement exist only for the duration of your multiplayer session and are not retained afterwards. The ten-second voice buffer described above is continuously overwritten and is discarded when you leave a room. Drawings are discarded the same way, and a drawing captured in a report screenshot is kept only as part of that report.

**Report evidence** — the voice clips and screenshots attached to a report — is deleted once a moderator has resolved that report. The decision itself, the reason, and who was involved are kept as a moderation record so bans can be justified.

Account details, your username, and your profile picture are kept until you ask us to delete your account.

Guest identities are deleted 24 hours after you leave, unless that guest has been banned.

Your comfort and control settings stay on your own device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Children

RoomWorks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us using the address below and we will delete it.
