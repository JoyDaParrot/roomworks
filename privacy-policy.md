# RoomWorks — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 November 2026**

RoomWorks is a multiplayer VR game. This explains what the app processes, why, who it's shared with, and how to get it deleted.

## What we process

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Display name, avatar appearance, position/movement | So other players see you | Sent to other players in your room |
| Account details (email, username) | Create and sign in to your account | Unity Gaming Services |
| Profile picture | Shown on your profile — a picture of your avatar, not a photo of you | Unity Gaming Services, cached on your device |
| Voice audio | Voice chat, and moderation if you're reported | Streamed live via Epic Online Services. Last 10 seconds held on-device, overwritten constantly, uploaded only if someone reports |
| Text chat, drawings | Chat and drawing with others in your room | Sent to other players, not stored |
| Report evidence (voice clips, screenshots) | So moderators can judge a report fairly | Created only when a report is made; stored by Unity Gaming Services |
| IP and connection data | Needed to connect you to a session | Processed by Epic Online Services; may be visible to other players in your room |
| Comfort and control settings | Remember your preferences | Stored on your device only |

## Microphone

We don't record conversations. The app keeps a rolling 10-second buffer on your device — overwritten continuously, uploaded only if someone reports. Mute anytime in-game, or revoke mic permission in your headset's settings.

## Reporting a player

Report someone from the social menu. Most reports capture 10 seconds of both voices, a screenshot from each viewpoint (game world only — no camera access), both usernames, and your reason. Reports go to moderators only; the reported player isn't told who reported them. Limit: one report per 30 seconds, one per player per 24 hours.

## Accounts

Optional — you can play as a guest. Sign in with **Discord**, **Google**, **Meta**, or **email and password**. We store only what's needed to identify you (ID, username, email); passwords are never stored in readable form. No real name, address, or payment details are collected. Guest identities are deleted 24 hours after you leave, unless banned.

## Drawings

Seen by others in your room, not stored, gone when you leave. Not auto-filtered like text chat, but still covered by moderation and reports.

## Moderation

Ban records, moderator notes, and report evidence are kept to enforce bans and review reports fairly. Visible to moderators only, never to other players.

## Third-party services

**Epic Online Services** — networking and voice chat. **Unity Gaming Services** — accounts, profiles, saved settings, reports. **Discord / Google / Meta** — only if you sign in with them. RoomWorks is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store.

## How long information is kept

Session data (voice, chat, drawings, position) isn't retained after your session. Report evidence is deleted once resolved; the decision itself is kept as a moderation record. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Guest identities are deleted 24 hours after leaving, unless banned.

## Children

Not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has given us information, and we'll delete it.
